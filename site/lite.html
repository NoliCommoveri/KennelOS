A real record keeper, at no cost, for a small program

Lite is the whole KennelOS core — dogs, pedigrees, pairings, litters, puppies, sales and money — sized for a hobby or starting program: up to 6 adult dogs and 2 litters. No trial clock, no card, no account.

What's included

Everything below is in the free download. It isn't a preview of the paid app — it's the same core, running the same way.

🐕 Dogs

  • One record per dog, from puppy to retirementLife stage changes on the same record, so the history never splits.
  • Registration details, colours, markings, microchip, photos
  • Health test results against your breed's panel
  • Multi-generation pedigree trees, drawn from your own dogs
  • Offspring, littermates and related records on every profile

🧬 Breeding

  • Unlimited pairings — plan them well ahead of time
  • Breeding dates, projected due date, confirmation
  • Whelp a pairing into a litter with a record per puppy
  • Weights, milestones and vet visits on each pup
  • Puppies never count against your dog limit

🤝 Sales & buyers

  • Log a sale against the pup: price, deposit, dates, status
  • Add the buyer inline while you're writing the sale up
  • Track deposits taken, balances due and go-home dates
  • Sale records survive after a dog leaves your program

💵 Financials

  • A full expense ledger — stud fees, vet, feed, supplies, travel
  • Sale income alongside it, so a season nets out
  • Categories you can actually report against at tax time
  • Export everything to CSV for your accountant

🔔 Today

  • Due dates, upcoming vaccinations and health checks
  • Pups reaching go-home age, and anything overdue
  • Reminders you set yourself, on any record
  • The one page to open with your morning coffee

📦 Your data

  • Full JSON backup, exported whenever you want it
  • CSV import for dogs, litters, pairings, sales and events
  • Every import previews what it will do before it commits
  • Works offline; installs to your home screen or desktop
  • Nothing is uploaded anywhere — there's no server to upload to

The limits, stated plainly

We'd rather you knew exactly where the edges are before you start typing.

6 adult dogs, 2 litters

The dog count is living adult dogs you own or co-own. Puppies don't count until they mature. A dog that has left your program doesn't count. A deceased dog doesn't count — and all of their records stay exactly where they are.

Pairings, puppies, sales, expenses and history events are unlimited in Lite.

What happens when you hit it

Adding a seventh adult dog, or a third litter, stops with a friendly prompt that offers the upgrade — and offers to export your backup on the way. Nothing you already entered is ever locked, hidden or taken away.

The same prompt appears when a puppy you kept grows up into an adult stage while you're at the limit.

What's not in Lite

These live in Pro. They're genuinely absent from the Lite download — not hidden behind a lock.

  • A Contacts section (buyers, vets, co-owners, partners)
  • Contracts beyond a logged sale
  • Stud services, incoming and outgoing
  • External and leased dogs
  • Reports — litters, placements, health tests, finances
  • Invoices, receipts and printable puppy records
  • Documents and file storage
  • Companion share-out and the Furever hand-off link
  • Feeding schedules and the Assistant

Curious what they look like? The live demo has all of it, already populated.

Ready when you are

Lite is a web page. Opening it is the whole install — then it works offline like any other app.
