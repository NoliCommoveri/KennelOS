Local-first, and we mean it literally

Most "cloud" breeding software is a database on someone else's computer that you rent access to. KennelOS is the opposite arrangement.

Where your data actually lives

In your browser's own storage, on the device you typed it into. It is not uploaded, not synced by default, not readable by us — we don't run a server that could hold it.

The trade-off, stated plainly: data on one device is only as safe as that device. That is exactly why export is a first-class feature, and why the app nudges you to take backups.

What that buys you

  • It keeps working when the internet doesn't.
  • No one can lose your records in a breach they had.
  • No monthly fee to keep reading what you wrote.
  • Export to JSON or CSV any day, for any reason.
  • If we vanished tomorrow, your copy still opens.
