Live demo · Free, no sign-up

The whole of Pro, with a kennel already in it

Reading a feature list is a poor way to judge software you'd keep years of records in. The demo is the real Pro app, pre-loaded with a realistic breeding program, so you can open a dog, walk a pedigree, whelp a litter and see a report on data that's already there.

Open the live demo See Pro pricing

What you'll find in it

A sample kennel with dogs across every life stage, real pedigrees behind them, litters at different points, sales in progress, and the paperwork that comes with all of it.

🐕

A populated Dogs list

Breeding stock, retired dogs, kept puppies and outside dogs, with health tests, photos and pedigrees that go back generations.

🧬

Litters at every stage

A planned pairing, a pregnancy with a due date, and a whelped litter with individual puppies, weights and vet visits.

🤝

Sales and buyers

Deposits taken, pups reserved, placements completed — each linked to the buyer's contact record and its paperwork.

📊

Reports with numbers in them

Litters, placements, health-test coverage, stud services and litter finances — run against the sample program.

🧾

The Pro-only paperwork

Contracts, stud services, invoices, printable puppy records and documents — the parts Lite doesn't ship.

🔔

A Today page with real items

Due dates, upcoming vaccinations and overdue reminders, so you can see how the daily view actually behaves.

It's read-only, on purpose

Click anything you like — open forms, change fields, try a delete. Nothing is saved, so the demo stays the same tidy kennel for the next person, and you can't break it.

Save and export are removed from the demo entirely. It's a shop window, not a workspace.

You bring nothing to it

No email, no account, no card, no download. The sample data loads itself when you open the page.
