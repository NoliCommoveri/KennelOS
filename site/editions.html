How the Lite limit actually behaves

Lite counts living adult dogs you own or co-own. Puppies don't count until they grow up, dogs that have left your program don't count, and deceased dogs don't count — their records all stay right where they are.

You are never locked out of what you already entered. Hitting the limit only stops you adding a seventh adult dog or an eleventh litter, and it says so with an upgrade prompt rather than a broken button.

Not sure which one you need?

Start on Lite. It's free, it's the same app, and everything you enter comes with you if you move up.

Upgrades

The easiest path for upgrading is using our cloud backup option to securely move your KennelOS Lite records to the Pro platform.

Alternatively, you may download a complete backup file in Lite, then import the file into Pro, to restore your records.
