KennelOS Furever · Free for families

The app your puppy families keep for years

Furever is a separate, free app for the people who take your pups home — a care schedule that knows the pup's age, reminders they'll actually act on, a place for records and photos, and training to work through. It's the same local-first deal: their data, their device.

Furever is in active development — features are still landing.

What a family gets

Not a marketing app for your kennel — a genuinely useful pet-care app they'd keep using in year three.

📅

A schedule that knows the age

Vaccinations, worming, checks and milestones project forward from the pup's date of birth, so nothing depends on the family remembering the interval.

✅

One-tap "done"

Marking something done moves it into a permanent care log, and the next occurrence schedules itself.

🐶

A profile per pet

Several pets, each with their own details, photo, records and history — with a family-wide "at a glance" view over all of them.

🎓

Training

Structured curricula to work through with the dog, with progress that carries across tracks.

📸

Photos & documents

A gallery for the growing-up pictures, plus somewhere to keep the vet paperwork, registration and contracts.

🔒

Private by construction

Their own storage on their own device. No account, no upload, and nothing about their pet is visible to you or to us.

For breeders: the hand-off (Pro)

KennelOS Pro can build a link that arrives with the pup already in it — so a family opens one text message and finds their dog, not an empty app.

  1. Fill in your kennel identity onceKennel name, your contact details, your vet.
  2. Pick the pup and add a personal notePlus the pickup plan — when, where, what to bring.
  3. Send the linkBy text or email, however you already talk to them.
  4. They open itThe pup's details, its history and your details are all there, and the care schedule is already running.

Why it's good for you

  • Families stay on the vaccination schedule you recommended
  • Your name and number are in the app they open weekly
  • Fewer "what was he due for again?" messages at 9pm
  • A resend updates their copy in place — nothing to re-enter

It's a separate app, deliberately

Furever isn't an edition of the breeder app and doesn't share its storage. Nothing a family writes in Furever comes back to you, and nothing else in your kennel goes out with the link — just that pup's packet.
