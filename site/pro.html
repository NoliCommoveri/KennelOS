How the license works

KennelOS has no server, so Pro isn't an account — it's a key. Here's exactly what that means day to day.

You get a key by email

Checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. They email you a license key; you paste it into Pro once, on each device you use.

It checks in, quietly

Pro confirms the key is still active when it can reach the internet. If it can't, it keeps working — 7 days on the yearly plan, 3 on monthly. Lifetime keys never expire; they run 3 months offline before asking you to connect once, with another month's warning after that.

Your data is never held hostage

Your records live on your device, not in our product. Export a full backup any time, and keep it whether or not you keep subscribing.

Why a subscription for an app that doesn't run a server?

Because the work is ongoing: new features, breed data, bug fixes, keeping up with browsers. A subscription is what funds a small product being maintained rather than abandoned — and if that isn't your preference, the lifetime option is there, and Lite stays free either way.
