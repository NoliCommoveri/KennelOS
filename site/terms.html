2. What KennelOS is

KennelOS is a record keeper for dog breeders that runs in your web browser and stores your records on your own device. Lite is free, with a limit on adult dogs and litters. Pro is paid, with no limits and more features. The demo is Pro with sample data, and saves nothing. Furever is a free app for puppy families. The online service is optional: every app works without it.

5. Keeping your records safe

Your records live in your browser's storage on your device. Clearing your browser's data, uninstalling the browser, or losing or resetting the device can erase them, and we can't recover what was only on that device. You are responsible for keeping your own backups: export a backup file regularly, and turn on cloud backup (and private backup, for your private details) if you want a copy held for you. Cloud backup reduces this risk; it doesn't remove it.

6. Cloud backup, private backup and your account

  • It's optional and free in Lite and Pro. You sign in with your email and a code we send you. Keep access to that email, and keep your devices locked: anyone who can read your email or open your unlocked device may be able to reach your backup.
  • Private backup is encrypted with a key we never have. If you lose your recovery code, every passkey and every unlocked device, nobody, including us, can open it. That's the point of it, and it means we can't help you get it back.
  • Fair use. Cloud backup is for one breeding program's records. We may limit storage, requests or documents to keep the service running for everyone, and we'll tell you in the app if you reach a limit.
  • You can delete it at any time from the app (Delete my cloud data).
  • It's provided as is. We work hard to keep it running and your backups intact, but we don't guarantee it will always be available or that no backup will ever be lost. Keep your own backup file as well.
