1. Who we are, and what this covers

KennelOS ("KennelOS", "we", "us") makes the KennelOS apps and runs the services behind them. This policy covers:

  • this website, kennelos.app;
  • the apps: KennelOS Lite (lite.kennelos.app), KennelOS Pro (pro.kennelos.app), the demo (demo.kennelos.app) and KennelOS Furever (furever.kennelos.app);
  • our online service at api.kennelos.app, which runs cloud backup, private backup and the online waitlist;
  • the waitlist pages families use, at apply.kennelos.app, and the emails sent from mail.kennelos.app.

Questions about this policy, or about anything we hold: email admin.kennelos@gmail.com. Use of KennelOS is also governed by our terms of use.

2. The short version

  • The apps keep your records in your own browser, on your own device. If you never sign in to cloud backup, we receive none of them.
  • Cloud backup is optional, free, and off until you turn it on. It copies your kennel records so you can get them back on a new device. Your contacts' phone numbers, emails and addresses, prices, Financials, contracts and private notes are never sent readable. With private backup on, they're encrypted on your device first, with a key we don't have.
  • We don't keep your email address, only a scrambled (keyed-hash) form of it, so we can recognise you when you sign in.
  • The online waitlist, if a breeder turns it on, shows families their own place and offers. We can read each family's name and email so we can email them; their application answers and messages are encrypted to the breeder.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no cookies, on this website or in the apps. We never sell or share anything about you for advertising.
  • You can delete everything we hold, from inside the app, at any time.

3. Your records, and other people's details in them

KennelOS is a record keeper. Your records will include details about other people: buyers, co-owners, vets, waitlist families. You decide what goes in, why, and who it's shared with, so for those details you are responsible for them (in data-protection terms, the controller). Where our service stores or sends any of them, we do it only on your instructions and only to provide the service to you: we don't read them, use them for anything of our own, or contact those people except to send the emails you ask for.

For your own account with us (your hashed email, devices, purchases), and for this website, we are responsible.

4. Using the apps without cloud backup

Everything you type is stored in your browser's own storage on that device. It is not sent to us. The apps contact other services only when you use a feature that needs them:

  • Pro's license key is checked with Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider. The app sends them your key and the name of this device (the name you give it, plus a short random id), so you can tell your activations apart. We never receive your key.
  • Dropbox (Pro), if you connect it: your backup file goes to a folder in your own Dropbox account, under Dropbox's own terms. We never see it.
  • Jotform (Pro), if you connect it: your Jotform API key stays on that device only, and the app asks Jotform, straight from your browser, for your list of forms and their fields. We never see the key or your forms.
  • Companion and Furever links (Pro) carry what you chose to share in the link itself. They don't pass through our servers.

The demo never contacts our online service at all, and nothing you do in it is saved.

5. This website

This website and the apps are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the network (IP) address of each visit and may keep it in its own logs for security; we don't receive those logs. The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics, tracking pixels or third-party scripts. The checkout buttons take you to Lemon Squeezy's site, where their privacy policy applies.

6. Cloud backup

Off unless you turn it on. You sign in with your email address and a code we email you.

What we keep

  • Your account: a keyed hash of your email address (not the address), when you joined, and for each device you sign in on: the name you gave it, when it was last used, and on Pro, the id of that device's license activation (so you can free it if the device is lost; it's useless without your license key, which we never receive).
  • Your kennel records: dogs (names, registrations, pedigree links, dates, microchip numbers, health-test plans), your kennels (including location, website and logo), pairings, litters, puppies, health and history events (without free-text notes), sales and stud services without their money, contract dates and types, feeding schedules, and your contacts' names and types.
  • Documents: health-test results, pedigrees and registration papers you've attached to dogs. Receipts, contracts and other documents stay on your device unless private backup is on.
  • Pro's waitlist: your waitlist settings (including the fee you charge and your payment instructions), the list's order and status, each family's status-page link, and each applicant's name and email. Nothing else from an application, and no family's payments.

What we can never read

Your contacts' phone numbers, email addresses and postal addresses; the prices, deposits, fees and payments on your sales, stud services and litters, and each waitlist family's payments; your Financials (income and expenses) and receipts; contracts and their terms; application answers other than name and email; and every free-text note, on any record. Without private backup they never leave your device. With it, they reach us only encrypted (section 7).

What we use it for

Only to keep your backup, give it back to you on your devices, and run the features you turn on. We don't read your records, sell them, use them for advertising, or use them to train AI models.

7. Private backup (optional, encrypted)

Off unless you turn it on, separately from cloud backup. With it on, each backup also carries a copy of all your records, private details, receipts and contracts included, encrypted on your device before it's uploaded, with a key that only your devices hold. We store that copy, its size, and when it was made. We can't open it, and neither can anyone who gets into our servers.

  • Your recovery code is shown once, on your device. We never receive it. We keep the vault key locked under it, which is useless without the code.
  • A passkey, if you add one, unlocks private backup on a new device with Face ID, a fingerprint or the device PIN. It lives in your device's password manager (for example iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager). Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device; we keep only the passkey's id, a random value it needs, the vault key locked under it, and the name you see in the list. A passkey here never signs you in.
  • Unlocking from another device passes the key between your two devices through our server, locked with the code you type on the other device. We delete the request when it's answered or after 10 minutes.

If you lose your recovery code and every passkey, and have no other unlocked device, we can't unlock your private backup for you. Your devices and file backups are unaffected.

8. Buying Pro

Pro is sold through Lemon Squeezy, who act as the merchant of record: they take your payment and billing details, handle tax and receipts, and email you your license key, under their own privacy policy. We never see a card number.

When you buy, renew, cancel or are refunded, Lemon Squeezy tells our service. We keep a keyed hash of the purchase email (never the address), which plan (monthly, yearly or lifetime), its status, and until when it gives you Pro. We never receive or keep your license key. This is how our service knows a signed-in account is Pro, which the online waitlist needs. If you bought with a different email from the one you sign in with, Link a Pro purchase email sends a code to that address, and typing it adds that address's hash to your account. If you never sign in to cloud backup, the hash is never matched to anything.

9. The online waitlist (Pro)

If you turn on Online list for a kennel, your device publishes a view of that waitlist to our service, so families can see their status and respond, and so the service can send your emails and make the moves you allow while your phone is off. It holds, readable:

  • About your kennel: its name, logo and time zone; your application form's questions, FAQ and notices; your fee wording and payment instructions; your email templates; and your Facebook Page link, if you add one.
  • The public list, if you share it: each family's first name and last initial, sex preference and the date they were added.
  • For each family: name, email, status and place on the list; offers and the pups eligible for them (call name, sex and colour); the fee amount and your payment instructions only while their fee is due, and whether and when you received it; their requests and your decisions; and the emails sent to them.
  • Litters and pairings you choose to show: the parents' call names and titles, and the dates you publish.

It never holds a family's phone number, address, other application answers, notes or payment details, or any price you haven't published. Taking the list offline removes the published view and every family's link and sign-in; Delete my cloud data removes everything.

You are responsible for having families' agreement to this, for the wording of what you send them, and for answering their requests about their information. The emails we send on your behalf go out in your kennel's name.

10. If you applied to a kennel's waitlist

The kennel you applied to decides how your application is used; please contact them first with any question about it. Our part:

  • Your application is encrypted in your browser to the kennel's key before it's sent. We can read only your name and email, which we need to send you a confirmation code and the kennel's emails. Cloudflare Turnstile may check that you're not a bot. An application whose code isn't typed is deleted after two days.
  • Our copy is a delivery copy. Once the kennel's device has received it and it is safely in their own encrypted backup, we delete it after 30 days. If they don't use that backup, it stays until they delete their data or stop using the online list.
  • Your status page opens with a private link or a code emailed to you. If you sign in, your browser remembers it for 90 days, in its own storage on that device; it isn't a cookie and isn't used for anything else.
  • What you do there (choosing a pup, passing, asking for a pause, leaving the list) is recorded for the kennel's device to apply, and deleted after 90 days. Messages you send are encrypted to the kennel, like your application.
  • Emails from the kennel come from an address at mail.kennelos.app. We keep each email's text so it can show on your status page; the text is deleted 90 days after it's sent, and the subject and date are kept for the kennel's records.
  • The public list, if the kennel shows one, shows your first name and last initial, the sex you prefer and when you joined. Nothing else.

You can leave a list at any time from your status page. To have your details removed, ask the kennel, or email us and we'll pass the request on and help.

11. Emails we send

Sign-in codes, codes to link a purchase email, application codes, and the waitlist emails a kennel sends. They're plain text with no tracking pixels or tracked links. We never send marketing email or newsletters, and we never email you unprompted about the service: notices appear inside the app. If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you.

13. How long we keep things

  • Backups: the newest one always, plus one per hour for the last day and one per day for 30 days. Older ones are deleted automatically, and so are documents no remaining backup uses. A backup's encrypted private copy goes with it.
  • Turning off private backup (Import/Export → Cloud backup → Private backup) deletes its locked keys at once, so nobody can unlock it again; the encrypted copies already made are deleted within 30 days.
  • Codes: sign-in and link codes expire after 10 minutes, family sign-in codes after 15. A signed-out device's record is deleted after 30 days.
  • Pro purchases: deleted 90 days after the purchase stops giving you Pro.
  • Waitlist: as in section 10: unconfirmed applications 2 days; delivered applications and messages 30 days after they're safely backed up; family actions 90 days; email text 90 days after sending.
  • Abuse limits: we count requests per email (hashed) and per network address (also hashed, never stored as-is), per hour, and delete the counts after a day.
  • Delete my cloud data (Import/Export → Cloud backup → Account) deletes your backups, documents, devices, waitlist data and account immediately. Your records on your devices are not touched.

14. Security

Everything travels over HTTPS and is stored encrypted at rest. Private details are encrypted on your device before they leave it. We don't log the contents of anything you send: never an email address, a code, a token or a record. Erasing a device, signing out your other devices and deleting your cloud data each ask you to confirm with a fresh sign-in. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach ever affected your information, we'd say so in the app and tell the relevant authorities as the law requires.

15. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and take your data: everything is in the app, and Import/Export saves all of it as a file. Email us for a copy of what our service holds about your account.
  • Correct it: edit it in the app; the next backup replaces the old copy.
  • Delete it: use Delete my cloud data, or email us.
  • Stop it: turn off cloud backup, private backup or the online list at any time.
  • Complain: to us first, if you like, or to your local data-protection authority.

We answer requests within 30 days. Because we don't keep your email address, we may ask you to confirm a code sent to it, so we know a request is yours. Where a data-protection law asks for a legal basis, ours is providing the service you asked for, and our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and free of abuse.

16. Where your data is processed

Our providers run worldwide networks, so your information may be processed in the United States and other countries with different data-protection laws from yours. We rely on each provider's own data-protection commitments for those transfers.

17. A lost or stolen device

If a device had cloud backup on, you can erase KennelOS on it from another device (Import/Export → Cloud backup → Your devices). The erase happens the next time that device opens KennelOS with an internet connection; it can't reach a device that stays offline.

18. If we ever stop running the online service

We'll say so inside the app well ahead of time. The apps keep working without it, and your records on your devices are untouched.

19. Children

KennelOS is made for breeders and isn't directed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you think a child has used our online service, email us and we'll delete what it holds.

20. Changes to this policy

If what we hold or how we use it changes, we update this page first and change the dates at the top. If a change matters to how your data is handled, we'll also say so in the app before it takes effect.

21. Contact us

Email admin.kennelos@gmail.com. We read every message.
